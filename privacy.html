Privacy Policy

Lyrics Studio Uploader · Last updated: November 12, 2026

1. Summary

Lyrics Studio Uploader is a personal tool used only by its owner, Annas Faza Maliki, to upload videos to the owner's own TikTok account. It does not collect data from other people.

2. Data the app uses

  • TikTok authorization data: after the owner signs in with TikTok, the app receives an access token, a refresh token, and the account's open ID. These are used only to upload the owner's videos and to read basic account info needed for uploading.
  • Videos and captions: lyric videos and captions created by the owner, which the app uploads to the owner's TikTok account as drafts.

3. How data is stored

Tokens and videos are stored only on the owner's own computer or private server. They are not stored on this website.

4. Sharing

The app does not sell, rent, or share any data with third parties. Data is sent only to TikTok, through TikTok's official APIs, to perform the uploads the owner requests.

5. Removing access

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time from TikTok's settings (Security and permissions, Manage app permissions). Stored tokens are deleted when the app is no longer used.

6. Children

The app is not directed at children and is used only by its adult owner.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated. The date at the top shows the latest version.
